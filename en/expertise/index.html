INTERNATIONAL EXPERTISE

Partner search. Direct negotiations. Transaction preparation.

SIMURG studies the market with your needs in mind. For example, when you are buying, we compare offers from manufacturers and suppliers, taking into account the order quantity, delivery costs, lead times and payment terms. You use this comparison to choose whom to continue negotiating with. SIMURG arranges direct discussions between the parties and helps prepare the transaction.

  • Where is there demand for your products?
  • Who should you talk to about cooperation?
