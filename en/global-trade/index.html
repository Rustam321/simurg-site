HOW SIMURG COORDINATES
INTERNATIONAL TRANSACTIONS
INTELLIGENT SOLUTIONS FOR YOUR TRANSACTION

You describe what you need. SIMURG weighs product requirements, payment terms and delivery dates together, assesses the risks and proposes ways to proceed. The buyer discusses commercial terms directly with the manufacturer or supplier. Once the parties reach agreement, a SIMURG manager coordinates document preparation, delivery and settlement according to the agreed plan.

OUR MISSION

To help companies pursue opportunities in international trade. To bring together participants, knowledge and resources to prepare and carry out transactions with due regard for the parties’ interests.

HOW WE WORK

You describe your needs through WEB PORTAL. AI helps clarify the request. The director then reviews it and appoints a Trade Officer, the manager responsible for the transaction. We draw up an implementation plan, agree it with the participants and support the work at each stage. In the workspace, the parties can see the latest agreements, documents and status updates within their access rights.

OUR ADVANTAGE

For example, the manufacturer may need an advance payment while the buyer needs deferred payment. SIMURG compares the delivery and payment schedules, assesses when funds will be needed and for how long, and asks financial partners what terms might be available. The parties can see the cost of each available option and agree suitable terms directly.

WHAT YOU GET

Direct dialogue with your counterparty, an agreed implementation plan and a manager responsible for the transaction. Participants have access to the documents, deadlines and open issues that concern their part of the work. If circumstances change, SIMURG helps assess the implications and agree on the next steps.

SIMURGCOORDINATION AND SUPPORT FOR INTERNATIONAL TRANSACTIONS

SIMURG helps coordinate transactions involving companies, public-sector buyers and participants in consumer markets. For each assignment, we identify the participants, define how they will work together and establish the steps for carrying it out.
