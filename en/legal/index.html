01

LEGAL TERMS OF THE TRANSACTION

When preparing a transaction, SIMURG helps identify the legal issues to consider when selecting participants, a route, delivery terms and settlement arrangements.

The countries where participants operate and the territories through which goods and funds pass are taken into account. Depending on the transaction, the review covers trade, transport, customs clearance, insurance, financing and contractual obligations.

Matters requiring a specialist opinion are referred to the relevant specialists under a procedure agreed with the participants. Any restrictions identified are taken into account when preparing the execution plan.

02

APPLICABLE REQUIREMENTS AND RESTRICTIONS

When preparing a transaction, the applicable requirements and the issues to check before execution are identified. The scope of the review depends on the goods, the participants, the countries involved and the transaction terms.

  • The status of the participants and the authority of their representatives.
  • Whether transactions involving the selected goods are permitted.
  • Export and import requirements.
  • Customs, foreign exchange and tax matters.
  • Sanctions restrictions and export controls.
  • Requirements for contracts, settlements, insurance and supporting documents.

SIMURG does not organise transactions by circumventing laws or sanctions restrictions.

03

ACCESS TO DOCUMENTS

The participant and the operator agree on a communication channel for exchanging documents. The participant may use the available functions of the SIMURG WEB PORTAL within the permissions granted to them.

The following documents may be provided depending on the participant’s involvement in the transaction and the agreed scope of access.

COMMERCIAL DOCUMENTS
Contracts, specifications, invoices, commercial offers and other documents that set out the transaction terms.
DELIVERY DOCUMENTS
Commercial invoices, packing lists, transport documents, certificates and other materials that accompany the goods in transit.
SETTLEMENT DOCUMENTS
Documents relating to payments and other financial events in the transaction, to the extent provided for by applicable requirements and the parties’ agreements.

Access is granted with due regard for confidentiality, trade secrets and the protection of personal data, banking information and other protected information. The materials available depend on the participant’s role and the terms of the agreement.

04

TRANSACTION SUPPORT AGREEMENT

Once your request has been reviewed, the operator is identified. This is the company with which you enter into a framework agreement. Before signing, the SIMURG manager responsible for your transaction (Trade Officer) provides the operator’s name and company details and presents the terms of support.

You and the operator sign a separate Statement of Work for each transaction. In it, you agree on the scope of work, timelines, budget and the parties’ obligations.

The parties agree on the following in the agreement:
  • The subject matter and scope of the services.
  • The rights and obligations of the parties.
  • How the parties will work together, including through the WEB PORTAL.
  • How documents will be provided and used.
  • Confidentiality terms.
  • Allocation of liability.
  • Governing law and dispute resolution procedures.
  • Conditions for suspending and ending support.

05

PARTICIPANTS’ OBLIGATIONS AND AGREED ACTIONS

Material documents, approvals and changes to the transaction terms are recorded during transaction support. Participants can follow progress through the materials available to them according to their role.

Each party’s obligations and liability are determined by the contracts it has entered into and the applicable requirements.

The company providing SIMURG services is responsible for its own obligations under the support agreement. The manufacturer, seller, buyer, carrier, financial institution, insurer and other participants fulfil their own obligations under their respective contracts.
