COORDINATED WORK BY TRANSACTION PARTICIPANTS

EXECUTION SUPPORT

The buyer agrees terms directly with the manufacturer or supplier. SIMURG helps the parties draw up an implementation plan. For each stage, we clarify the deadline, who is responsible, which documents are required and when payments linked to delivery are due.

The manager responsible coordinates communication between the parties and tracks the agreed stages. In the workspace, participants can see documents, status updates and open issues within their access rights. If work departs from the plan, we help assess the implications and agree on the next steps.

PARTICIPANT COORDINATION
TRANSACTION SUPPORT

TRADE

We help the parties agree on the goods, quantities, terms and stages of execution. We record agreements and changes that require a decision by the participants.

LOGISTICS

We coordinate delivery preparations with transport and warehousing partners. We track deadlines, cargo status and issues affecting delivery.

FINANCE

We compare the settlement schedule with the stages of delivery. We coordinate document preparation and the exchange of information with the financial partners involved.

RISKS

We monitor circumstances that may affect execution. We report deviations from the plan and help the parties agree on a response.

DOCUMENTS

We coordinate document preparation and approval. We record current versions and statuses and arrange access to the materials relevant to each participant’s work.

RESPONSE TO CHANGES

For example, if the supplier postpones shipment, the SIMURG manager checks how this will affect delivery and payment. The manager proposes options to the parties and records the decision they reach.

AGREED RESPONSIBILITIES

The tasks assigned to SIMURG and the participants’ obligations are set out in the agreements and documents for the transaction.
