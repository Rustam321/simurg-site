DIRECT COOPERATION AND TRANSACTION SUPPORT

You describe what you need. SIMURG compares purchasing, delivery and settlement terms, assesses the risks and proposes options for the transaction. The buyer discusses terms directly with the manufacturer or supplier. Once terms are agreed, a SIMURG manager coordinates their implementation.

YOUR TRANSACTION WORKSPACE

Terms, documents, stages and deadlines are all in one place. Each participant has access to the information they need for their work.

DIRECT DIALOGUE WITH THE MANUFACTURER OR SUPPLIER

The buyer discusses the products, price and delivery terms directly with the manufacturer or supplier. SIMURG arranges these discussions and helps the parties agree on how to carry out the transaction.

AGREED TERMS AND CLEAR DEADLINES

The parties can see the current agreements, outstanding issues and progress. Each next action has a person responsible and a deadline.

A DEDICATED MANAGER FOR YOUR TRANSACTION

Your transaction is supported by a Trade Officer, the SIMURG manager responsible for it. This manager coordinates the participants’ work, monitors deadlines and helps resolve issues as they arise.
